Definitions

  • Net revenue = Revenue - Total expenses
  • Revenue = Rooms revenue + cleaning fees + taxes + cancellation fees
    Revenue is meant to be all the money paid by guests. Even though guests pay up front, for the purposes of this report I pretend they pay evenly each non-checkout day. So if a guest has a $200 reservation checking in on May 1st and checking out on May 3rd, $100 will count towards May 1st, $100 will count towards May 2nd, and $0 will count towards May 3rd.
  • Total expenses = rent + cleaning fees* + channel fees + taxes** + bento charges + credit card fees
    *"Cleaning fees" will become "Cleaning charges" once we finally get a handle on how much the cleanings actually cost. For the moment we make the assumption that cleaning charges equal cleaning fees.
    **Note that taxes occurs in revenue, because we collect taxes from guests, but also in expenses, because we turn right around and remit it to the government.
  • Market rent = the rent we pay landlords. Even though we pay rent once a month, for the purposes of this report I pretend we pay rent daily. So if rent is $2,800 and you query from February 1st to February 3rd your report will say $200, which is $100 for the 1st, $100 for the second, and as always, the report is "up to but not including" the end of the date range.
  • Cleaning fees = a charge that occurs once per reservation, typically between $60 and $100. Like revenue, these are pro-rated.
  • Channel fees = whatever money Airbnb or Bookingcom takes out of our reservations, typically a percentage of the nightly rate charges. These too are pro-rated.
  • Taxes might be, for instance, city taxes we collect on a Booking.com reservation as a separate line item.
  • Cancellation fees are the charges we didn't refund to guests for their cancelled reservations due to our cancellation policies.
  • ADR = Average Daily Rate, a.k.a. the average nightly revenue for occupied rooms. In other words, revenue / total occupied nights
  • Occupancy rate = total occupied nights / total available nights
  • RevPAR = ADR * Occupancy rate, a.k.a. revenue / total available nights
