Definitions

  • Net revenue = Total income - Total expenses
  • Total income = all the money paid by guests. Even though guests pay up front, for the purposes of this report I pretend they pay evenly each non-checkout day. So if a guest has a $200 reservation checking in on May 1st and checking out on May 3rd, $100 will count towards May 1st, $100 will count towards May 2nd, and $0 will count towards May 3rd.
  • Total expenses = rent + cleaning fees + channel fees + taxes + utilities
  • Market rent = the rent we pay landlords. Even though we pay rent once a month, for the purposes of this report I pretend we pay rent daily. So if rent is $3,000 and you query from February 1st to February 15th, your report will reflect $1,500.
  • Cleaning fees = a charge that occurs once per reservation, typically between $60 and $100. Like "total charges", these are pro-rated.
  • Channel fees = whatever money Airbnb or Bookingcom takes out of our reservations, typically a percentage of the nightly rate charges. These too are pro-rated.
  • Taxes might be, for instance, city taxes we collect on a Booking.com reservation as a separate line item.
  • ADR = Average Daily Rate, a.k.a. the average nightly revenue for occupied rooms. In other words, total charges / total occupied nights
  • Occupancy rate = total occupied nights / total available nights
  • RevPAR = ADR * Occupancy rate, a.k.a. total charges / total available nights
