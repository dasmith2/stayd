Notes

  • Net = Total charges - market rent - cleaning fees - channel fees - internet - utilities
  • Total charges = all the money paid by guests. Even though guests pay up front, for the purposes of this report I pretend they pay each day. So if a guest has a $1000 reservation from May 1st to May 10th and you query from May 1st to May 5th, your report will reflect $500.
  • Market rent = the rent we pay landlords. Even though we pay rent once a month, for the purposes of this report I pretend we pay rent daily. So if rent is $3,000 and you query from February 1st to February 14th, your report will reflect $1,500.
  • Cleaning fees = a charge that occurs once per reservation, typically between $60 and $100. Like "total charges", these are pro-rated.
  • Channel fees = whatever money Airbnb or Bookingcom takes out of our reservations, typically a percentage of the nightly rate charges. These too are pro-rated.
  • Charges - fees = Total charges - cleaning fees - channel fees - internet - utilities. Pro-rated.
  • ADR = Average Daily Rate, a.k.a. the average nightly income for occupied rooms. In other words, (charges - fees) / (total occupied nights)
  • Occupancy rate = total occupied nights / total available nights
  • RevPAR = ADR * Occupancy rate, a.k.a. (charges - fees) / (total available nights)
